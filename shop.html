ADAM FALP


Is an artist, illustrator and cartoonist based in London.


His work explores underground culture, psychedelia, absurdity and contemporary Britain. His comics have appeared in independent and underground publications internationally, alongside his own successful self-published titles including Electric Chair and Smokin’ Bedroom. He also draws and edits for Underground Kingdom Comix, an independent British comics collective featuring cartoonists from across the UK underground scene.
